Overview
The SAP Certified Associate - Security Administrator certification exam verifies that the candidate has a general understanding of the core knowledge required of a Security Administrator in the area of SAP system security. It proves that the candidate has a basic understanding about SAP authorization and security concepts in SAP S/4HANA Public Edition and Private Edition and can put this knowledge into practice as a member of a project or security team.
